About Born to Fight

Born to Fight is a free turn-based MMA career simulator you play in the browser. You build a fighter - attributes, style, nationality, look - then run training camps, hire a coaching team, sign sponsors, and fight your way from the amateur circuit up through six promotions to a world title.

Fights play out exchange by exchange: pick strikes and combinations, shoot takedowns, work the ground, and adjust your game plan from the corner between rounds. The judges score on real criteria - significant strikes, control, damage, knockdowns. Around you, a living division of rivals fights on its own, the rankings shift, and champions come and go.

Features

  • Six promotions to climb, a live 16-fighter division, and a pound-for-pound style rating
  • Turn-based fights with combos, corner tactics, cuts and long-term brain health
  • Coaching staff, sponsors, weight management, rivalries with memory
  • Achievements, career records, fight replays, and a Hall of Fame
  • Retire and coach a protege who inherits part of your legacy
  • English and Polish
