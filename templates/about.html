About Born to Fight

Born to Fight is a free MMA simulator and career game - a turn-based fighting sim you play in the browser, no download or account required. You build a fighter - attributes, style, nationality, look - then run training camps, hire a coaching team, sign sponsors, and fight your way from the amateur circuit up through six promotions to a world title.

Fights play out exchange by exchange: pick strikes and combinations, shoot takedowns, work the ground, and adjust your game plan from the corner between rounds. The judges score on real criteria - significant strikes, control, damage, knockdowns. Around you, a living division of rivals fights on its own, the rankings shift, and champions come and go.

Features

  • Six promotions to climb, a live 16-fighter division, and a pound-for-pound style rating
  • Turn-based fights with combos, corner tactics, cuts and long-term brain health
  • Coaching staff, sponsors, weight management, rivalries with memory
  • Achievements, career records, fight replays, and a Hall of Fame
  • Retire and coach a protege who inherits part of your legacy
  • English and Polish

Frequently asked questions

Is Born to Fight a real MMA simulator?
Yes - it's a turn-based MMA simulator and career game. You don't just pick a winner; you call strikes, takedowns and ground exchanges round by round while a judging system scores the fight on real criteria.

Is it free to play?
Yes, completely free, with no account and nothing to install. It runs in any modern browser, desktop or mobile.

Is this more like an MMA manager game or a fighting career sim?
Both - you manage the business side (camp, coaching staff, sponsors, weight cuts, contracts) and you personally fight every round of every bout, so it plays like a career sim with manager-game depth underneath.

Can I play as a boxer, wrestler or grappler, not just a striker?
Yes - pick a fighting style (striker, brawler, wrestler, grappler or well-rounded) when you create your fighter; each has its own passive bonuses and AI tendencies for opponents who use it.

Do I need to create an account?
No. A private, anonymous save is tied to your browser automatically.
