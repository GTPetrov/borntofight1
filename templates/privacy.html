Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What this site is

Born to Fight is a free browser game. There are no user accounts.

Cookies we set

  • bt_player - a random id that keeps your saved careers separate from other visitors' on the same browser. It contains no personal information.
  • lang - remembers your interface language (English or Polish).
  • bt_consent - remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice.

Your game saves are stored on the server, keyed to the bt_player id. They hold only game data (your fighter, record, and progress).

Analytics

The server keeps aggregate counters - total page requests per day, which pages are visited, and how many distinct browsers were active - to understand usage. These are not tied to your identity.
